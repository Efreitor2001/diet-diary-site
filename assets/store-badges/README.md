## Language selection

| Website language | RuStore | Google Play | AppGallery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Russian | Russian | Russian | Russian |
| English | Russian original; no official English asset found | English | English |
| Simplified Chinese | Russian original; no official Chinese or English asset found | Simplified Chinese | Simplified Chinese |

Use official artwork in the page's language when available, with English as the
fallback for Chinese pages. The [English RuStore resources](https://www.rustore.ru/help/en/developers/advertising-and-promotion/button-rustore/rustore-button)
and [Chinese RuStore resources](https://www.rustore.ru/help/zh/developers/advertising-and-promotion/button-rustore/rustore-button)
both link to the same Russian `logo-color-dark.svg`; their embedded previews also
contain Russian text. Keep that original until RuStore publishes a translated
asset. Alternative text is localized on every page.

The shared stylesheet displays each badge at a 216-pixel width and preserves
its original aspect ratio. The original RuStore artwork is proportionally taller;
it is not stretched or cropped to match the other artwork. Each link has
20 pixels of clear space on each side (at least one quarter of the tallest
badge's height). The badges wrap on narrow screens and stay centered on mobile.
Google Play and AppGallery artwork are localized for each page. The legacy
English `appgallery.png` remains available, but the pages use the explicit
language filenames. Google trademark attribution appears below the links.
Stylesheet URLs are versioned so previous cached badge sizing is replaced.

All links lead to the app's store listing. Tracking parameters from the Huawei
sharing URL are omitted. Assets are served by this website rather than hotlinked.
