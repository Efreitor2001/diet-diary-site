The shared stylesheet keeps the artwork at its original aspect ratio, at a
64-pixel height, with 16 pixels of clear space on each side. Google Play is at
least as tall as the other store badges. Google Play artwork and alternative
text are localized for each page; the original RuStore Russian and AppGallery
international artwork are shared between pages. Google trademark attribution
appears below the download links.

All links lead to the app's store listing. Tracking parameters from the Huawei
sharing URL are omitted. Assets are served by this website rather than hotlinked.
