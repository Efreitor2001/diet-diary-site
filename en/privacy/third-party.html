Yandex Mobile Ads 8.6.0

Provider: Yandex (YANDEX LLC and applicable affiliates under its policy). Purpose: ad loading and display, ad-interaction measurement and technical SDK operation.

Data: IP address and network-request information; device, operating-system and app information; available advertising and technical identifiers; ad requests, impressions and clicks; technical diagnostics. The app does not supply diary contents, Google email or Google account identifier in advertising parameters.

When: while verified ad removal is not active, the SDK may initialize when the app or ad placements open without a separate prior consent dialog. The app sets setUserConsent(false), setLocationTracking(false) and setAppAdAnalyticsReporting(false). Location tracking and App Ad Analytics reporting are disabled; precise and approximate location permissions are not requested. These settings are not a general prohibition of network processing. After verifying a valid paid license, the app hides ads, stops its own ad requests and releases cached advertising objects; this does not guarantee that all background work of an SDK already running in the session stops. There is no separate advertising-consent switch.

Provider rules: privacy and requests; SDK data description. Retention and international processing are determined by the provider; clearing your diary is not a deletion request to Yandex.

The advertising SDK dependencies are described separately below. Data categories describe the relevant components' capabilities, not a claim that all are active on every device. Including a library does not by itself establish activation or data transmission. The app does not call these supporting APIs directly or supply diary records to them.

AppMetrica 8.5.1

Provider: Yandex (YANDEX LLC and applicable affiliates). Component purpose: technical diagnostics and app/ad measurement; included as a Yandex Mobile Ads dependency.

Data categories: technical device, OS and app information, crashes and performance; available SDK and advertising identifiers. Advertising events depend on enabled features. The app does not configure separate diary-action analytics or send custom events containing diary contents.

Access method and condition: relevant SDK modules obtain technical information through Android and library APIs while operating and may send it to Yandex services. This build disables App Ad Analytics and location tracking before advertising initialization. This does not establish that all internal SDK diagnostics are disabled.

Yandex Privacy Policy · AppMetrica data categories · Ads integration and reporting controls.

Varioqub adapters 0.6.0

Provider: Yandex (YANDEX LLC and applicable affiliates). Component purpose: connecting SDK configuration and experiments to an analytics system. The advertising dependency includes Varioqub adapters; the developer does not configure separate diary experiments.

Data categories: when the relevant integration is used — project identifier, technical Varioqub identifier, configuration parameters and activation events. Custom parameters require explicit input from the integrating code; the app does not supply diary contents, Google email or tokens.

Access method and condition: the adapter connects the calling SDK to AppMetrica; network configuration updates and event reporting depend on initialization and calls by the relevant integration. The diary code does not call Varioqub directly; adapter presence does not establish that all experiment features are active.

Yandex Privacy Policy · Varioqub integration documentation.

Google Advertising ID 18.1.0

Provider: Google LLC and applicable affiliates. Component purpose: providing an advertising identifier for advertising components and ad measurement; included through an AppMetrica dependency.

Data categories: Google Advertising ID (GAID) and the limit-ad-tracking setting. These are separate from the Google email and account identifier used for Drive.

Access method and condition: when requested, the library calls device Google Play services APIs. Availability depends on services, user settings and the AD_ID permission, which is present in the build. Restriction may result in a zero identifier or unavailable access. Retrieving the identifier and subsequently processing it through an advertising SDK are separate operations; dependency presence does not prove transmission of a nonzero GAID.

Google Privacy Policy · Advertising ID documentation.

Google App Set ID 16.1.0

Provider: Google LLC and applicable affiliates. Component purpose: technical identification of an app or app set on a device for analytics, separate from the advertising identifier.

Data categories: App Set ID and its scope — an individual app or a developer's set of apps. It is not a Google account or diary content.

Access method and condition: a calling component requests the identifier through the App Set ID library API. Its scope depends on the installation source and available services; the documentation specifies app scope for installation outside Google Play. The library is available to third-party SDKs in this build, but the diary code makes no separate request.

Google Privacy Policy · App Set ID documentation.

Google Play Install Referrer 2.2

Provider: Google LLC and applicable affiliates. Component purpose: obtaining installation-source information for third-party SDK attribution.

Data categories: the installation referral string, click and installation-start times, app version at first installation, and Instant experience interaction indicator, where supplied by the store.

Access method and condition: the client library queries the device's Google Play Store service. A supported store and available information are required; including this library in the Huawei build does not imply that every AppGallery user has Google Play or an available referrer. The diary does not call the library directly.

Google Privacy Policy · Install Referrer documentation.

Google sign-in and Drive; AppAuth Android 0.11.1

Recipient: Google LLC and applicable affiliates under Google's policy. Purpose: optional account connection, backup snapshots and merging the diary across devices.

Data and timing: after you choose to connect — OAuth requests, email, account identifier and tokens; during synchronization — diary contents, settings and change metadata. Data goes directly to Google. Access is limited to openid, email and drive.appdata. The first synchronization follows sign-in, then runs manually or automatically according to your setting.

When a purchase exists, transferable settings include the purchase number and technical details used to verify it. They can be included in Google Drive and JSON exports at your choice. To check a restored purchase, the app connects directly to Robokassa over the internet. Card details and payment-form email are not added to backups; do not publish the purchase number or a full backup.

AppAuth is an open-source client library for browser sign-in and token exchange and refresh. In this integration, it contacts Google; the OpenID organization is not a separate diary-receiving server. AppAuth documentation.

Google Privacy Policy · Permissions and protection · Google Limited Use · Disconnection and deletion. The app does not select the country where Drive data is stored or apply end-to-end encryption to JSON snapshots.

Robokassa: optional payment

Starting a new payment requires signing in to Google, enabling automatic synchronization and successfully syncing the diary with Google Drive. Before opening the payment form, the app performs a fresh sync; if it fails, the app opens the Google screen with an explanation and a sign-in button. Immediately after payment is confirmed, the app saves the purchase on the device and synchronizes it with Google Drive. If synchronization fails, the purchase stays on the device and the app offers to retry synchronization. This is a condition for starting a purchase, not a technical link between the purchase and a Google account. Sign-in is optional for the free diary; a purchase already verified on the device continues to work offline.

Recipient: ROBOKASSA LLC and the providers of your selected payment method. Purpose: a one-time RUB 149 payment for permanent ad removal and issuing payment documents; there is no subscription or automatic recurring payment.

Data and processing method: the official Robokassa Android SDK opens the payment web form and checks payment status directly with Robokassa. You enter payment details and email in the form. Robokassa also receives purchase parameters and technical request data. Diary code does not read the completed payment details from the form. Diary contents, health information and Google data are not sent to Robokassa. The purchase number is stored with the diary and backups you choose for a new check; an unfinished invoice is kept only in private app storage on the device.

When: only when you use an available purchase feature; including the SDK in a build does not mean live payments are already enabled. Terms and processing are described in the Robokassa Privacy Policy and seller's offer (in Russian). Clearing the diary does not erase mandatory transaction records or payment documents.

File recipients you select

SDKs and services do not automatically receive the same data: food records are not sent to advertising or Robokassa, and Google synchronization starts after connection.
